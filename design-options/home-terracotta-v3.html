Swipe sideways to pan the workflow

  1. Lead enters the workflow.
  2. A LinkedIn connection request is sent.
  3. If accepted, a LinkedIn message is sent. If not accepted within 5 days, an email is sent instead.
  4. If the message is seen, the lead goes to the score check. If not seen after 2 days, an email is sent.
  5. If the email is opened or clicked, the lead goes to the score check. If not, the lead is paused and recycled after 30 days.
  6. If the engagement score is above 70, a call task is created for the owner. Otherwise the lead is paused and recycled after 30 days.

How it works

From workflow to next action, automatically.

02

Flightpath runs the first touches
