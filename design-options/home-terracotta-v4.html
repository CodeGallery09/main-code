Swipe sideways to follow the journey

  1. A lead enters the workflow.
  2. A LinkedIn connection request is sent.
  3. Once accepted, a LinkedIn message follows.
  4. If there is no reply, a follow-up email goes out.
  5. Opens, clicks and replies are watched.
  6. The engagement is scored: 70 and above goes to a call, the rest keep nurturing.
  7. A call task is created for the owner.
  8. The owner takes over the conversation.
  9. The result is benchmarked against your range.

How it works

From workflow to next action, automatically.

Four moves, from picking a motion to routing the next step by behaviour.

01

Pick a proven workflow

Start from the Workflow Library: outbound, inbound and re-engagement motions with channel order and guardrails already set.

Outbound · Inbound · Re-engageChannel order preset
02

Flightpath runs the first touches

Each lead moves through LinkedIn and email in a coordinated order. Touches never collide, and nothing is sent twice from two tools.

One sequence per leadNo double sends
03

It listens for engagement

Accepts, opens, clicks and replies are read as signals in real time, across every channel the workflow uses.

04

The next action is routed, not scheduled

Instead of “wait three days, send step 4”, the engine checks each branch and picks the one that matches what the lead actually did.
